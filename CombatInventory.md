# Combat and spell concept inventory

## 1. Types of damage

Seven, one per school.

1. Physical
2. Holy
3. Fire
4. Nature
5. Frost
6. Shadow
7. Arcane

## 2. Unit resource types

Health, plus five powers, of which a unit uses one.

1. Health
2. Mana
3. Rage
4. Focus
5. Energy
6. Happiness

## 3. Sources of damage

Six.

1. Main-hand melee
2. Off-hand melee
3. Ranged
4. Spell
5. Periodic tick
6. Environment — falling, drowning, fatigue, lava, slime, fire

## 4. Damage mitigation

Nine. A proposed order is:

1. Immunity — the school does not touch the victim
2. Avoidance — see section 5
3. Damage-taken modifiers — flat and percentage reductions on the victim
4. Block — a fixed amount removed by a shield
5. Glancing — a fraction of the blow survives
6. Armour — physical only, capped at three quarters
7. Resistance — magic only, capped at three quarters
8. Absorption — shields take the blow until they are spent
9. Splitting — part of the remainder moves onto another unit

## 5. Outcomes a blow can be shown as

Eleven, and this list is fixed by the client, which holds exactly these words and
displays one of them over the unit.

1. Miss
2. Resist
3. Dodge
4. Parry
5. Block
6. Evade
7. Immune
8. Deflect
9. Absorb
10. Reflect
11. None — the blow lands and a number is shown instead of a word

A blow that lands shows its amount. Every other outcome shows its word.
